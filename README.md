# 🌍 LifeDex

### Discover. Collect. Explore.

**LifeDex** is an Android discovery app that turns everyday exploration into a rewarding experience.

Scan objects around you, save your discoveries, earn XP and build your personal collection!

## ✨ Features

- 📷 **AI Object Recognition** – Scan objects using your camera.
- 🔍 **AI Suggestions** – Get suggestions for detected objects.
- 📚 **Discovery Collection** – Save and revisit your discoveries.
- ⭐ **XP System** – Earn experience points for discovering new things.
- 💎 **Rare Discoveries** – Earn bonus XP for selected rare discoveries.
- 🏆 **Leaderboard** – Compare XP with other explorers.
- 👤 **User Accounts** – Sign in using email or Google.

---

## 🎮 How LifeDex Works

1. Open LifeDex and sign in.
2. Go to the Scan section.
3. Point your camera at an object.
4. Capture and identify it.
5. Select a suggestion or enter the discovery details.
6. Save your discovery and earn XP.
7. Explore your collection and leaderboard.

## 📱 Compatibility

- Platform: Android

---

## 🔐 Privacy

LifeDex uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for account and discovery data.

Image recognition is performed on-device using Google ML Kit. Captured images are processed for recognition and are not uploaded to Firebase by the current scanning implementation.

Do not submit passwords or other private information through public GitHub issues.
